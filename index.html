1. Introduction

CS Teacher Prep is a Computer Science preparation application designed to provide practice and preparation resources for examinations such as Bihar STET and BPSC PGT (Computer Science).

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the CS Teacher Prep application.

2. Information Collection

The current version of CS Teacher Prep does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the main study and practice features of the application.

Users may voluntarily provide information when using external contact or support options, such as email or messaging services. Users are encouraged not to provide sensitive personal information through such channels unless it is necessary for the purpose of their communication.

3. Locally Stored Data

CS Teacher Prep may store certain information locally on the user's device to provide app functionality.

This may include:

  • User-created questions and self-designed practice sets.
  • Information required to manage locally available app features.
  • Temporary local access information associated with set-unlock functionality.

Such locally stored information is used to provide the relevant functionality of the application.

4. Advertising Services

CS Teacher Prep may display banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Google and its advertising partners may collect, use, or process certain information in connection with advertising, subject to their applicable privacy policies, terms, and user settings.

For more information about how Google handles information, please refer to Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Third-Party Services and External Links

The application may provide access to third-party services or external content when users choose features such as advertisements, email, messaging, YouTube playlists, or other external links.

These third-party services and websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices. Users should review the applicable policies of those services when interacting with them.

6. Notifications and Reminders

CS Teacher Prep may provide practice reminders or other app-related notifications when the corresponding feature is enabled or used.

Notification permissions are controlled through the user's device settings where applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

CS Teacher Prep is an educational and examination preparation application. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through external contact, support, feedback, or messaging channels.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to support the operation and protection of locally stored app data.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, absolute security of information cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data Sharing

CS Teacher Prep does not require users to create an account for its main study features.

Information may be processed by third-party services used by the application, such as advertising or external services, according to the respective service's policies and applicable settings.

10. Your Choices

Users can control certain permissions and settings through their Android device settings, including notification permissions and other applicable permissions.

Users may also choose whether to interact with optional external services and links provided through the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be made available through the application's Privacy Policy page and/or the public Privacy Policy webpage.
